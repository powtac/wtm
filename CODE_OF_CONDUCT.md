# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project free from harassment, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and remove sensitive local-machine data from reports.
- Accept maintainers' decisions on safety, scope, and project direction.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel. Maintainers may edit,
reject, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and proportionately. Public
launch is blocked until this repository names a monitored private contact channel.

This compact policy is based on the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
